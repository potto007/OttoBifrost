# OttoBifrost artwork

Tagline: **The Veil Between Worlds Grows Thin**

Created with the built-in image generation tool from the OttoBifrost README and the OttoLens, OttoStash, and OttoFuel artwork.

## Files

- `ottobifrost-title.png`: widescreen title graphic with the full tagline.
- `ottobifrost-icon-master.png`: original full-resolution square icon.

## Direction

A gnarled wooden portal reveals a distant fjord and mountain settlement before the viewer crosses its threshold. This reflects the README's live destination preview and direct passage when the destination is ready. Weathered iron circular arrows, amber lighting, cold blue-gray scenery, faceted ivory Nordic lettering and copper bevels match the sibling Otto artwork. Lettering is generated to match the raster references; there is no separate editable font file.

## Title prompt

Use case: ads-marketing.
Create one finished widescreen 16:9 high-resolution title banner for the Valheim mod "OttoBifrost".
Input images: Image 1 OttoLens banner, Image 2 OttoStash banner, Image 3 OttoFuel banner. All three are STYLE AND TYPOGRAPHY REFERENCES, not subjects to copy. Faithfully match this existing Otto mod family: cinematic Nordic fantasy realism, physically textured weathered hammered iron two-arrow circular crest, charcoal and cold misty blue-gray pine forest, copper and rich amber edge lighting, premium dimensional cracked ivory lettering with copper-orange bevels and dark extrusion. Match the distinctive angular Nordic blackletter letterforms in OttoFuel and OttoStash, including their Otto prefix.
Repository-derived subject: the mod turns a Valheim circular wooden portal into a live window onto its destination, allowing a player to see the far landscape before stepping straight through when ready. Convey a tangible, transparent threshold joining two distant places. 
Composition: shared family layout, a complete large round crest on the LEFT occupying about 36 percent of width; title prominently on RIGHT with generous safe margins. Inside the outer two-arrow hammered iron ring put a recognizable ancient Valheim-style circular portal made from gnarled dark timber, carved amber-glowing runes and a slim shimmering fiery-amber edge. Its OPEN interior reveals a sharply legible distant cool mountain valley, pale blue fjord, pine trees and a small warm-lit Norse hall on the far shore. A simple stone path crosses the near threshold and visibly continues into the distant scene; perspective and difference in lighting make the opening clearly a view through to somewhere else, not a landscape painted on a medallion. Only delicate wisps of translucent veil at the portal's edge; keep the destination clearly visible. Make this portal silhouette strong enough to extract as a small icon. The outer iron arrow crest follows the exact family language, two large complete arrowheads, warm copper rim light, balanced heft.
Backdrop outside the emblem: an ancient Norse portal clearing beside a rustic timber shelter, cold dark pines and distant ruined mountain fortress under a brooding sky, worn stone ground lit by the portal's amber glow. Epic and mysterious, same physical richness and dark/warm contrast as the references. Background supports the wordmark and does not obscure it.
Text verbatim: "OttoBifrost" (O-t-t-o-B-i-f-r-o-s-t), one line. Heavy sculpted ivory Nordic lettering with fine cracks, copper bevels, very legible and prominent. Beneath it a thin refined horizontal divider with a small Nordic knot, then exact tagline "THE VEIL BETWEEN WORLDS GROWS THIN" in a single line of modest widely spaced uppercase ivory serif letters matching the sibling banners. Keep the entire slogan legible, safely within the right-hand title area; no final punctuation. No other words.
Constraints: This is OttoBifrost, not OttoLens/OttoStash/OttoFuel. Do not include eye/ravens, chest emblem, campfire emblem, tech UI, loading bars, modern machinery, sci-fi metal Stargate, rainbow highway, comic-book characters or watermarks. Do not fill the opening with opaque flames or a vortex: seeing the other side is the key feature. Keep complete emblem, arrowheads, title and tagline uncropped. Single polished final banner, no contact sheet.
